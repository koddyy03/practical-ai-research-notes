# Practical AI Research Notes

AI tools, grants, programs, and workflows are often shared faster than their
conditions can be verified. This repository collects concise, source-based
research notes that separate official facts from interpretation and hype.

## Current notes

- [Codex for Open Source: fact-check](docs/codex-for-open-source.md)
- [Codex for Open Source: eligibility self-check](docs/codex-for-oss-self-check.md)

## How notes are verified

1. Find the official announcement, documentation, or application page.
2. Record the date checked because programs and pricing change.
3. Separate confirmed facts, reasonable interpretations, and unsupported claims.
4. Link directly to primary sources.
5. Do not publish credentials, account details, private repository information,
   or personal application data.

## Add a note

Copy [the research template](templates/research-note.md), add the date and
primary sources, then keep the conclusion short enough to act on.
